Three Life Domains

Body tracks workouts, meals, sleep. Mind tracks quests, journal notes, daily check-ins. Money tracks income, spending, your real bank balance. Each levels independently — plus an overall character level.

⚡
×3.5 max

The Loop

Log activity → earn XP → tier up. Streaks multiply your XP up to 3.5×. A 21-day workout streak doubles every XP gain. Bosses are milestones (e.g. 5-day workout streak) that grant big XP when defeated. Tiers unlock real perks like subscription discounts.

🏦

Money & Bank

Tap the ☰ menu (top-left) → Connections → 🏦 Bank to securely link your account. Gold mirrors your real available balance — 1 gold = $1. Transactions auto-categorize. Old history fills the charts; nothing inflates your gold.

☰

Everything lives in the menu

The ☰ icon in the top-left opens the full menu: History, Friends & Leaderboards, Rules, What’s New, Connections, Account, Additional Resources (recipes & guides), and About & Help. To replay this tour later, open Help and tap Replay the welcome tour. The exit icon on the right signs you out.
